 After finishing with one flower, the bee zips to another. Some of the pollen from her body rubs off
 onto the second flower. This simple act is called pollination. Pollination allows plants to make seeds
 and fruits. Without it, many plants could not grow new life.

Pollination is not just good for plants and bees; it helps humans too. Apples, strawberries,
 cucumbers, almonds, and many other foods need pollinators. In fact, scientists say one out of every
 three bites of food we eat exists because of pollination.
